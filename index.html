Hello! I'm Wanjohi Ian.

For a long time, I have been curious about the systems behind the technology we use every day - how applications communicate, how data moves between services and how software works at scale.

That curiosity led me to teach myself software development over the past 2 years, beginning with UX & Product Design. It turned out to be a strategic starting point because it taught me about how software should feel and behave from the user's perspective. Over time, though, I found myself more drawn to what was happening underneath the surface, and moved into Backend Development, where I have kept my focus since.

I'm particularly interested in complex technical challenges, especially with systems that have many moving parts and evolving business requirements (the kind that enterprise systems produce). I'm also drawn to backend work that requires systems thinking - beyond building software, designing it to be reliable, maintainable and scalable.

Currently, I'm looking for an opportunity to turn the self-directed learning into hands-on professional experience. I'm hoping to join a team that offers strong mentorship and a collaborative learning enviroment, where I can sharpen what I have learned by working on real production systems. I'm starting with C# and ASP.NET Core, but remain flexible on technologies. What I am ultimately interested in are the fundamental principles that underpin good software development.
